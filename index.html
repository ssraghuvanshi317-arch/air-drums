How to use
  1. Wrap the two stick tips in bright, different coloured tape (e.g. neon pink and neon green). Plain, non-matching background, good light.
  2. Prop the phone 1–1.5 m in front of you so both sticks and their full stroke are in frame.
  3. Tap Start, then Pick stick A and tap the tip on screen. Repeat for B.
  4. Turn on Show mask: only the tip should light up. Adjust tolerance until it does.
  5. Drag Hit line to where your strokes bottom out. A hit fires when a tip crosses the line moving down. Pads left→right: Hi‑hat, Snare, Tom, Crash.
  6. Prediction fires a hit early if the tip will cross the line before the next frame. It cuts about one frame of lag. Raise it if hits feel late, lower it if you get false hits.
